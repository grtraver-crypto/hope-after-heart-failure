Why This Book

Written by Someone Who Has Been There

Greg R. Traver survived three "widow-maker" heart attacks and two separate heart failure episodes, including roughly three weeks intubated in an intensive care unit. This is his real account of the recovery that followed — not a fictional or illustrative example.

  • Written by a two-time heart failure survivor — not a clinician
  • 10 chapters covering body, mind, spirit, and community
  • Guided meditations and reflective workbook exercises
  • Practical strategies for cognitive fog and emotional recovery
  • Faith-based wisdom woven with evidence-based insights
  • A companion for the entire journey — from diagnosis to living whole
